About the App

Layers of Knowledge is an intelligent spaced repetition app built on the science of the Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve. It helps you retain any information — facts, vocabulary, concepts, professional knowledge — through perfectly timed review notifications.

  • Science-based spaced repetition (1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 90, 180 days)
  • Custom reviewing schedules
  • Smart notifications with pause & resume
  • Powerful fuzzy search across all your knowledge
  • Tag management for organized learning
  • Data export & import with full backup support
  • Multilingual support (English, French, Arabic)
  • 100% offline — your data stays on your device

Frequently Asked Questions

How does spaced repetition work?

Spaced repetition is a learning technique based on the Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve. The app sends you review reminders at scientifically optimal intervals (1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 90, and 180 days) to help you move information into long-term memory.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All your data stays on your device. We don't require an account, don't collect any personal information, and don't have access to your knowledge items.

Can I pause notifications?

Yes! You can pause all notifications for a custom duration (vacation mode). When you resume, all your review schedules are automatically adjusted so you don't miss anything.

Can I create custom review schedules?

Yes. In addition to the default schedule, you can create your own reviewing schedules with custom intervals tailored to your learning needs.

Can I export and import my data?

Yes! You can export all your knowledge items as a JSON file for backup or transfer, and import them on any device running the app.

Can I relearn something I've already completed?

Yes! Use the Relearn feature to restart any item's review cycle from the beginning. This is perfect for information that needs reinforcement.
