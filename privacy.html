Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

Layers of Knowledge ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

The short version: We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

Layers of Knowledge does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or registration required
  • No personal data collected (name, email, phone, etc.)
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No cookies or web tracking technologies
  • No advertising identifiers

Data Storage

All data you enter into the App (knowledge items, tags, schedules, settings) is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server or third party. We never see or process this data.

Notifications

Layers of Knowledge uses local notifications to remind you of scheduled reviews. These notifications are generated and managed entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Layers of Knowledge does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app functions entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Deleting items within the app permanently removes them from your device
  • Uninstalling the app removes all associated data
  • You have complete control over your data at all times

Children's Privacy

Layers of Knowledge does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
